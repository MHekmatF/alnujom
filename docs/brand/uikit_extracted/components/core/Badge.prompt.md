**Badge** — small pill status markers; the gold `featured` and green `verified` badges are Al Nujom's two key trust signals.

```jsx
<Badge variant="featured">مميّز</Badge>
<Badge variant="verified" icon="badge-check">موثّق</Badge>
<Badge variant="sale">للبيع</Badge>
<Badge variant="rent">للإيجار</Badge>
<Badge variant="glass">3 / 12</Badge>
```

- Variants: `featured` (gold), `verified` (green), `purpose` (accent), `sale`, `rent`, `glass` (over-photo), `neutral`.
- Not tappable. Use `glass` over imagery; pair color with text/icon (never color-only).
